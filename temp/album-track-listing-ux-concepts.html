Track numbers and discs.

Three ways to add track numbers and multi-disc structure without making the existing album view feel heavier. In every option, a single-disc album stays free of disc headers or controls.

The quietest option: track numbers sit in a stable left rail, with disc headings only where a release has more than one disc.

Single-disc behavior

No “Disc 1” label and no disc selector. Option 1 and 2 show only the numbered track rows.

1 Blue Morning ▶

Sorting rule

Disc ascending first, then track ascending. Disc 1 track 1 through 4, followed by Disc 2 track 1 through 3.

Metadata fallback

If a track number or disc number is missing, show the title and duration normally. Never infer a disc when the album contains only one disc.
